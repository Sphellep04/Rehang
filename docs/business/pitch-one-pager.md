## Rehang: Namibia's verified marketplace for preloved fashion

**Clear your closet. Earn from it.**

### Problem
Namibians already sell clothes on Facebook and WhatsApp, but posts get lost, there's no way to search by size, and scams are common. Of the __ people we surveyed, __% have been scammed or know someone who has, and __% said fear of scams stops them buying preloved clothes online.

### Solution
A mobile-first web app just for clothes, where:
- every seller is verified with an ID and a selfie
- listing takes under two minutes, with a mode for adding many items at once
- buyers filter by size, brand, condition, town and price
- meet-ups happen at safe spots, confirmed with a code, and later the buyer's money is held until the item arrives

### Market
No dedicated, verified fashion marketplace exists in Namibia. Yaga (South Africa), Vinted and Depop show the model works. Starting in Windhoek, then Swakopmund and Walvis Bay, then Oshakati and Ongwediva.

### Business model
Free to list. 8% seller commission and a N$5 buyer protection fee on paid orders. Boosts and ads later. Break-even at about 700 sales a month, expected around January 2028.
