# Financial model

This replaces the single break-even figure in the original plan with a month-by-month view. It shows **when money actually starts coming in** and **how much funding is needed until then**.

> Every number here is a planning assumption to test, not a market fact. Update the assumptions table as real data arrives, and the rest follows.

## The gap the original plan missed

The original plan assumed commission on every sale. In practice:

1. **Phase 1 (Nov 2026 – Mar 2027) earns nothing.** Buyers pay sellers directly at meet-ups, so Rehang has no money to take a fee from.
2. **Founding sellers pay zero commission** for 6 months. If that period starts at public launch, it covers April to September 2027, when founding sellers make up much of the supply.
3. **Not every sale will be paid in the app**, even after held payments go live. Some buyers will still pay cash at meet-ups.

**Fix adopted in this plan:** charge the **N$5 buyer protection fee from the day held payments go live** (brought forward from Phase 2), including on founding sellers' items. It pays for dispute handling, which starts at the same time, and it gives Rehang income while founding sellers pay no commission.

Revenue per paid sale = N$5 buyer fee + N$14.40 commission × (1 − founding sellers' share).

### What this shows

- **Monthly break-even arrives around launch month 10 (January 2028)**, at about 700 sales a month.
- **Funding needed after launch: about N$45,000** (the lowest point of the cumulative column), on top of the N$10,000–20,000 before launch. **Plan for about N$55,000–65,000 in total**, from own funds, a grant, an incubator or a small investor.
- December is a strong month for clothes, but also a slow month for building and support. Plan for both.

## Break-even sensitivity

Monthly costs of N$11,500, after the founding-seller period ends, with 85% of orders paid in the app:

| Scenario | Rehang earns per sale (after the in-app share) | Sales a month to break even |
|---|---|---|
| Base: 8% + N$5 fee, N$180 average | N$16.49 | **about 700** |
| Original plan: 8%, no fee, every sale in the app | N$14.40 | about 800 |
| 8%, no buyer fee | N$12.24 | about 940 |
| 5% + N$5 fee | N$11.90 | about 970 |
| N$120 average item, 8% + N$5 | N$12.41 | about 930 |

**Takeaways:** the buyer fee and getting buyers to pay in the app matter as much as the commission rate. Push in-app payment with things cash can't offer: protection, ratings that only count for paid orders, and courier options.

## Levers if the numbers fall short

1. Shorten the founding sellers' free period to 3 months, or cap it (for example, the first N$5,000 of sales).
2. Launch boosts (N$10–30) earlier, once there are more than 2,000 active listings.
3. Set a minimum commission of N$5 for items under N$60.
4. Keep costs flat: the founder moderates until reports pass 20 a day, and creators are paid in store credit.
