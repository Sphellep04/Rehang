# Giving to children's homes

Members can give items to vetted children's homes. It is built and switched off. Set `donations_enabled` to `true` in `app_config` once at least one home has been checked.

## How it works

1. An admin adds a home on `/admin/donations`, after visiting or speaking to it. Homes are hidden until "Show on the Give page" is ticked.
2. A member opens `/donate/give`, picks one of their own listings (draft or live) and a home, and may add a short note. The item comes off sale. A member can have up to 5 gifts waiting at once.
3. The team is notified. A moderator accepts the gift once the home confirms it can use it, or declines with a reason (the item then returns to the member's listings).
4. After the handover the moderator marks the gift handed over. The item becomes `donated` and the member is told.
5. The public page shows only totals: items given and homes helped.

## Safeguarding rules

- Homes have no accounts. A home's contact details are visible to staff only. Members never see them.
- Members never receive a home's address and a home never receives a member's address or phone number. The Rehang team arranges every handover.
- Nobody contacts the children. There is no messaging between members and homes.
- Items only. Rehang does not pass on money.
- Only clean clothes in good condition. The team may decline anything else.

## To decide before switching on

- Who collects, and where: a drop-off at a safe meet-up spot, a courier, or collection by the home.
- Which homes to start with. Visit each one, ask for registration details, and agree a named contact.
- Whether to publish photos of handovers. Never include children's faces or names.
- Whether the privacy policy needs a line about gifts (it stores who gave what, for staff only).
