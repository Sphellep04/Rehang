# Brand guide (starting point)

The brand basics to settle in **Week 3** of Phase 0. Everything here is a proposal to test with the founding sellers.

## Name

**Rehang**: a garment is "hung again" and gets a second life with someone new.
- Always written **Rehang** (capital R, one word). Never "ReHang" or "Re-hang".
- Say it "ree-hang".

## Taglines

| Tagline | Use for |
|---|---|
| **Clear your closet. Earn from it.** | Main tagline. Seller recruitment |
| **Hang it again.** | Short sign-off, logo lock-up, stickers |
| **Verified sellers only.** | Trust messaging aimed at buyers |

Pick one main tagline after testing both in the WhatsApp test posts and the Instagram bio.

## Voice

**Friendly, honest, local.**

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Your items go live once you're verified. Usually less than 24 hours." | "Your submission is pending review." |
| Use N$ and Namibian towns and places | Use US$ or copy the wording of overseas sites |
| Short sentences, plain English | Jargon: "P2P", "escrow", "C2C" |
| Say what happens next | Blame the user |

Room for Oshiwambo and Afrikaans greetings in marketing later. The app stays in English at launch.

## Logo concept

- A simple **hanger mark**, with the hook forming a circular "re" arrow, meaning reuse.
- Works as a single colour, and is readable at 32 px (app icon, favicon).
- Brief for a student designer: 3 concepts, one refinement round, deliver SVG and PNG, app icon, and a WhatsApp and Instagram profile image.

## Colour palette (proposal)

Inspired by the Namib: warm sand, a deep night sky, and a bright accent for action.

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary / action | Rehang coral | `#E4572E` |
| Text and dark backgrounds | Night ink | `#1F2A44` |
| Light background | Namib sand | `#F4E9DA` |
| Surface | White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Trust / verified | Verified green | `#2E8B57` |
| Warning | Amber | `#F2A541` |

Check contrast: coral on white must meet WCAG AA for large text and buttons. Use night ink for body text.

## Typography

System fonts in the app (fast, no extra data). One display typeface for the logo and marketing only, for example a rounded geometric sans such as Poppins or Nunito from Google Fonts.

## Photography

- Real people, real Namibian settings: Windhoek streets, campus, markets.
- Natural light. No heavy filters.
- Item photos: plain background, hanger or flat lay (see the [photo rules](../product/listing-standards.md#photo-rules)).
