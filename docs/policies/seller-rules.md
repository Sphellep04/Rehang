# Seller rules

The short, friendly version shown to sellers during onboarding. It links to the [terms of use](terms-of-use.md) for the full legal version.

## The 8 rules

1. **Be verified.** Your listings go live once your ID check is approved. It usually takes less than 24 hours.
2. **Only sell what you own.** No stolen items, and nothing you're selling for someone else without their permission.
3. **Fashion only.** Clothes, shoes, bags and accessories. See the [prohibited items](prohibited-items.md) list.
4. **Real photos, honest condition.** Photograph the front, back and care label, and show every flaw. Choose the correct [condition grade](../product/listing-standards.md#condition-grades).
5. **Keep it in the app.** Don't share phone numbers or social handles, and never ask for deposits or upfront delivery fees. Chat and offers stay on Rehang, so we can protect both of you.
6. **Honour your deals.** Once you accept an offer, confirm the meet-up within 24 hours and turn up. Cancelling or not showing up gives you a [strike](../product/orders-meetups-and-delivery.md#strikes).
7. **Meet safely.** Use the safe meet-up spots, only in daylight. Let the buyer check the item before they pay and give you the code.
8. **Be respectful.** No harassment, no pressure, no discrimination.

## Pricing tips
- Check what similar items sold for. The app shows a suggested range beside the price box.
- Items priced within the suggested range sell faster.
- Make bundle offers for several items from the same buyer (built-in bundle discounts come in Phase 2).

## Shipping (from public launch)
- Ship within **3 days** of payment and add the tracking number in the app.
- Packaging: fold the item clean, put it in a plastic bag, and write the order number on the parcel.

## Fees
- Listing is **free**.
- Commission of [8]% applies only when a paid order completes. **Founding Sellers pay zero commission** until [date].

## What happens if rules are broken
Listings removed → warning → strikes → temporary suspension → permanent ban. Fraud leads to an immediate ban and may be reported to the police.
