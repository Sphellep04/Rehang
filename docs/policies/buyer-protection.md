# Buyer protection and disputes

> Draft for lawyer review. ⚖️ Confirm alignment with Namibian consumer protection requirements.

## Pilot stage: meet-up and pay in person

When you pay the seller in person, **your protection is checking the item before you pay.**

1. Meet at the safe spot you booked in the app, during daylight.
2. Check the item: size, condition, flaws, label.
3. Only when you're happy, pay the seller and read out your **meet-up code**.
4. If the item isn't what was listed, don't pay and don't share the code. Tap **"Report a problem"** on the order.

Rehang can't refund payments made in person. We can act against sellers who mislead buyers, from strikes up to bans.

## After held payments launch

When you pay in the app, **Rehang's payment partner holds your money until you confirm the item is as described.**

### You're covered if

| Problem | Covered |
|---|---|
| Item never arrived, or the seller didn't show up | ✓ Full refund |
| Wrong item sent | ✓ |
| Item significantly not as described (wrong size, undisclosed damage, wrong brand, condition clearly worse than the grade) | ✓ |
| Counterfeit sold as genuine | ✓ |

### You're not covered if

- You changed your mind, or it doesn't fit even though the listed size was correct
- You paid outside Rehang
- The flaw was shown or described in the listing
- You confirmed the order as OK, or the 48-hour window passed

### How to open a dispute

1. Within **48 hours** of delivery or meet-up, tap **"Something's wrong"** on the order.
2. Choose a reason and upload **at least 2 clear photos**.
3. The seller has **48 hours** to respond.
4. A moderator decides within **3 working days**:
   - **Full refund:** you return the item. The seller pays for return delivery if they were at fault.
   - **Partial refund:** you keep the item and get part of your money back.
   - **Release:** the seller is paid.
5. Both of you can still leave a rating.

The buyer protection fee (N$[5] per paid order) pays for this service. It isn't refunded unless the order is refunded in full.

## Misuse

Repeated false disputes lead to strikes and can lead to losing buyer protection.
