# Prohibited items

Rehang is **fashion only**. Anything not on the allowed list, or anything below, gets removed.

## Allowed
Clothing, shoes, bags, and fashion accessories (belts, hats, scarves, jewellery, sunglasses, watches), for women, men and kids, including traditional and occasion wear and uniforms.

## Not allowed

| Category | Examples | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Counterfeits and replicas | "AAA", "1:1", "inspired by" branded items, fake logos | Removed. Strike. Ban for repeat offenders |
| Stolen goods | Anything the seller doesn't own. Items with security tags still on | Removed. Account suspended. May be reported to the police |
| Used underwear | Worn underwear, bras, shapewear | Removed |
| Used swimwear | Swimwear that has been worn | Removed. **New with tags or sealed swimwear is allowed** (proposed. Confirm in the open decisions) |
| Hazardous or unhygienic items | Items with mould, bedbugs or unwashed stains, or items recalled for safety reasons | Removed |
| Official and restricted uniforms | Police, military or security uniforms and insignia | Removed |
| Non-fashion items | Phones, electronics, furniture, cosmetics, perfume, cars, animals, services | Removed (stays focused) |
| Weapons and dangerous items | Knives (including decorative knives sold with outfits), pepper spray | Removed |
| Offensive items | Hate symbols. Content that is sexually explicit or promotes violence | Removed. Strike or ban |
| Misleading listings | Stock photos, photos of a different item, "mystery bags" without photos of the contents | Removed. Warning |
| Wildlife products | Items made from protected species (ivory, certain skins) | Removed ⚖️ confirm the relevant Namibian and CITES restrictions |

## Notes

- **School uniforms** are allowed. So are corporate uniforms if logos are removed or the company allows resale.
- **Kids' items** must meet the same hygiene rules as adult items.
- **High-value brands** (over N$1,500) go through a brand photo check and get the "Photos reviewed" label. It isn't a guarantee of authenticity.
