# Seller interview guide

Used in **Week 2** (7 to 13 October 2026). Goal: 10 interviews with people who sell clothes online in Namibia now (Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram).

## Setup

- **Length:** 25–30 minutes, in person or on a WhatsApp call.
- **Who:** a mix of closet clearers, returns sellers and side-hustle resellers (see README section 3). At least 3 of each if possible.
- **Thank-you:** a founding-seller spot, or airtime or data worth about N$30.
- **Rule:** ask about what they *did*, not what they *would* do. Past behaviour predicts; opinions flatter.

## Opening (2 min)

> "Thanks for your time. I'm working on an idea to make selling clothes online safer and easier in Namibia. There are no right answers. I want to hear how you do it now, including what's annoying. Is it OK if I take notes?"

## 1. Their story (8 min)

1. Tell me about the last item you sold online. What was it, and where did you post it?
2. Walk me through it, from deciding to sell to getting the money.
3. How long did the whole thing take? Which part took longest?
4. How did you decide the price?
5. How did the buyer pay? How did the item get to them?

## 2. Pains (8 min)

6. What's the most annoying part of selling clothes online?
7. Tell me about a time a sale went wrong. What happened?
8. Have you had time-wasters, no-shows or scam attempts? How did you handle them?
9. How do you decide whether to trust a buyer?
10. What have you tried to make it easier? (for example templates, a separate Instagram page, bulk posting)

## 3. Money and logistics (5 min)

11. Which payment methods do you accept? Which do you prefer, and why?
12. Do you ever deliver or send items to other towns? How?
13. What would you pay, or give up, to never deal with a scammer again?

## 4. Concept reaction (5 min)

Only now, describe Rehang in two sentences:

> "An app just for clothes, where every seller and buyer is verified, you can list many items fast, and the buyer's money is held until they get the item. Listing is free, and Rehang takes a small fee only when something sells."

14. What's your first reaction?
15. What would make you *not* use it?
16. Would you list your items there? How many to start?
17. Would you share your ID and a selfie to get a "verified" badge? Why or why not?

## Closing (2 min)

- "Anyone else I should talk to who sells clothes?" (ask for 2 names)
- "Can I add you as a founding seller for our WhatsApp test in Week 3?"

## Synthesis (end of Week 2)

1. Put every pain on a sticky note (or a spreadsheet row), one pain per note.
2. Group similar notes and count how many interviewees mentioned each group.
3. Rank the top 5 pains. Check each one against the MVP features. Anything painful that the MVP doesn't solve goes into README section 19, "Open decisions".
4. Write a one-paragraph summary for the Week 4 go/no-go review.
