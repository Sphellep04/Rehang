# WhatsApp test playbook

Used in **Week 3** (14 to 20 October 2026). A manual version of Rehang, run on WhatsApp, to prove that people will buy from verified sellers before any code is written.

**Target: 30 items listed, at least 10 completed sales in 7 days.** The results feed directly into the [go or no-go checks](../../README.md#19-go-or-no-go-checks).

## Setup (Week 2)

1. Create a WhatsApp Community called **"Rehang Closet Test"** with two groups:
   - **Announcements** (admin-only): item posts. Keeping it read-only keeps the feed clean, like the app.
   - **Chat**: questions and community.
2. Recruit **10 founding sellers**, 3 items each, from the survey and interviews.
3. "Verify" each seller manually: a quick video call, or meet in person and see their ID. Don't store copies of IDs at this stage.
4. Collect photos from sellers following the photo rules in [listing standards](../product/listing-standards.md): front, back, label, flaws.
5. Invite buyers from the survey waiting list and the founder's own network. Aim for 80–150 members.

## Item post format

Post each item as one message with 3–4 photos:

```text
🧥 #R012  Mr Price denim jacket
Size: UK 12 / M  ·  Condition: Very good
Colour: Blue  ·  Town: Windhoek (Klein Windhoek)
Price: N$180
Seller: Thandi ✅ verified
To buy or make an offer: reply "R012" to the Rehang admin in a direct message
```

- Every item gets an ID (`R001`–`R030`) so it can be tracked.
- Buyers message **the founder, not the seller**, so every message and offer can be counted. The founder connects buyer and seller once a price is agreed.

## Handling a sale

1. The buyer messages "R012" and asks a question, or makes an offer.
2. The founder checks with the seller and agrees the price.
3. The founder arranges a **daytime meet-up at a safe spot** (see [orders and meet-ups](../product/orders-meetups-and-delivery.md#safe-meet-up-spots)) and sends both sides a 4-digit code.
4. At the meet-up, the buyer checks the item, pays the seller (cash or instant EFT) and tells the seller the code. The seller sends the code to the founder, which confirms the sale.
5. The founder marks the item SOLD in the group and logs it in the tracker.

This mirrors the app's meet-up code flow, so the test also checks whether the process makes sense to people.

## What to record

Use [`whatsapp-test-tracker.csv`](whatsapp-test-tracker.csv), with one row per item. Also keep:

- **Question log:** every question buyers ask (these become the FAQ and listing fields).
- **Scam and risk log:** anything suspicious, such as requests to pay a deposit, fake payment proof or no-shows.
- **Daily numbers:** new members, messages, offers, sales.

## After the test

Send a short message to each buyer and seller who took part:

1. How easy was it, on a scale of 1–5?
2. What almost stopped you?
3. Would you use an app that works like this? Yes / Maybe / No
