# Phase 0 survey

Used in **Week 1** (30 September to 6 October 2026). Goal: 50 responses from people in Namibia, mostly Windhoek.

## How to run it

- Build it as a single Google Form (free, works well on phones, low data use).
- Question 0 sends people to the seller or buyer section. Anyone who does both can fill in both.
- Keep each section to 10 questions so it takes under 3 minutes.
- Share in WhatsApp status, campus groups (NUST, UNAM), work groups and the Facebook groups listed in the README.
- Put the waiting-list sign-up at the end of both sections.

## Question 0 (routing)

**Which describes you best?**
- I have clothes I'd like to sell → Seller section
- I buy (or would buy) preloved clothes → Buyer section
- Both → Seller section, then Buyer section

---

## Seller section (10 questions)

1. **Which town do you live in?** Windhoek / Swakopmund / Walvis Bay / Oshakati / Ongwediva / Other (type it)
2. **Your age:** Under 18 / 18–24 / 25–34 / 35–44 / 45+
3. **Have you sold clothes in the last 12 months? Where?** (tick all) Facebook Marketplace / Facebook groups / WhatsApp / Instagram / Market stall / Consignment or thrift shop / I haven't sold any
4. **About how many items in your closet do you no longer wear?** 0 / 1–10 / 11–30 / More than 30
5. **What has stopped you selling them?** (tick all) Takes too long to post / Scammers and time-wasters / Don't know what price to ask / No buyers see my posts / Delivery or meeting up is a hassle / Never thought about it / Other
6. **Have you ever been scammed or had a buyer not pay?** Yes / No / Not sure
7. **How would you like to be paid?** (tick all) Cash at a meet-up / Instant EFT / FNB eWallet / MTC AwehPay or other mobile money / Paid out to my bank account
8. **Would you verify your identity (ID photo plus a selfie) if it made buyers trust you more?** Yes / Maybe / No
9. **If listing were free and Rehang took about 8% only when your item sells, would you list?** Yes, definitely / Probably / Not sure / No
10. **Can we contact you about becoming a founding seller? (zero commission for 6 months)** Yes, here's my WhatsApp number: ___ / No thanks

**Counts toward Gate 1:** a "Yes, definitely" or "Probably" on Q9. Anyone who also leaves a number on Q10 is a founding-seller lead.

---

## Buyer section (10 questions)

1. **Which town do you live in?** (same options as above)
2. **Your age:** Under 18 / 18–24 / 25–34 / 35–44 / 45+
3. **Have you bought second-hand clothes in the last 12 months? Where?** (tick all) Facebook / WhatsApp / Instagram / Thrift or consignment shop / Market or bales / I haven't
4. **How much do you usually spend on one preloved item?** Under N$100 / N$100–250 / N$250–500 / Over N$500
5. **What stops you buying preloved clothes online?** (tick all) Fear of scams / Can't try it on / Bad or fake photos / Hard to find my size / Delivery cost or hassle / Hygiene worries / Nothing stops me
6. **Have you, or someone you know, been scammed buying online?** Yes / No / Not sure
7. **What would make you trust a seller most?** (pick up to 2) ID-verified seller / Reviews from other buyers / Money held until I get the item / Meeting in a safe public place / Being able to return or dispute
8. **How would you prefer to get your item?** Meet-up in my town / Courier to my door / Post office / Pick-up point at a shop
9. **Would you pay about N$5 extra so your money is held until the item arrives as described?** Yes / Maybe / No
10. **Would you buy from verified sellers on an app like this?** Yes / Maybe / No. *Join the waiting list: ___ (WhatsApp number or email)*

**Counts toward Gate 1:** a "Yes" on Q10.
