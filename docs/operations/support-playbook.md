# Support playbook

## Channels

| Channel | When | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp Business | From the pilot | Mon–Sat 08:00–18:00. Auto-reply outside these hours |
| Email (support@) | From the pilot | Reply within 1 working day |
| In-app help centre | Once the same questions come up repeatedly (Phase 2) | Always |

Target: first reply **within 1 working day**, and within 4 hours for anything involving safety or an order in progress.

## Tone

Friendly, honest, local. Short sentences. Use the person's name. Never blame. Say what happens next and when.

## Saved replies (WhatsApp Business quick replies)

**/welcome**
> Hi {name}! 👋 Thanks for reaching out to Rehang. How can we help today?

**/verify-wait**
> Thanks for submitting your ID, {name}. Our team checks every seller by hand to keep Rehang safe. It usually takes less than 24 hours. Your items will go live automatically once you're approved, and we'll send you an SMS.

**/verify-rejected**
> Sorry, we couldn't approve your ID this time because: {reason}. You can resubmit from Profile → Verification. Tips: good light, the whole document in the photo, no glare.

**/off-app**
> For your safety, please keep chats and payments inside Rehang. We can only protect orders that stay in the app. Sellers who ask for a deposit or to move to WhatsApp are breaking our rules. Please report them with the ⚑ button.

**/no-show**
> Sorry they didn't show up. Please tap "They didn't show" on the order. The other person gets 24 hours to respond, then we'll sort it out. Your item or offer will be free again.

**/not-as-described-meetup**
> If the item isn't what was listed, you don't have to pay. Please don't share your meet-up code, and tap "Report a problem" on the order so we can follow up with the seller.

**/fees**
> Listing on Rehang is free. We only charge a small fee when an item sells through the app. Founding Sellers pay no commission until {date}.

**/delete-account**
> You can delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete account. Any orders still in progress need to finish first. Want us to walk you through it?

## Escalation

| Situation | Escalate to | How fast |
|---|---|---|
| Safety threat, violence or a stalking concern | Founder. Advise the user to contact the police (10111) | Immediately |
| Suspected fraud or a stolen ID | Founder | Same day |
| Data or privacy request | Founder (data protection contact) | Within 2 days, reply within 30 |
| Media or police enquiry | Founder only | Same day |
| Bug that blocks orders | Founder (developer) | Same day |

## Weekly review

Every Friday: tag each conversation by topic (verification, fees, meet-up, bug, other). The top 3 topics become help-centre articles or product fixes.
